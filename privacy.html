Comprehensive Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 2026

Welcome to BoostSERP ("we," "our," or "us"). As a premier provider of search engine optimization (SEO) and digital marketing services, we understand that your privacy is of paramount importance. This comprehensive Privacy Policy is designed to inform you—our valued clients, website visitors, and partners—about how we collect, use, maintain, protect, and disclose your personal and non-personal information when you visit our website or utilize our SEO services.

By accessing or using the BoostSERP website, you agree to the terms and practices outlined in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, your choice is not to use our website. We are committed to safeguarding your digital footprint while we work to boost your digital presence.

1. The Information We Collect

To provide you with industry-leading SEO solutions, we must collect certain information. We gather data from you in two primary ways: information you provide to us directly and information we collect automatically as you navigate through our site.

A. Personal Information You Provide to Us

When you interact with BoostSERP—whether you are requesting a free SEO audit, signing up for a newsletter, filling out a contact form, or purchasing our digital marketing packages—we may ask you to provide specific personally identifiable information (PII). This may include, but is not limited to:

  • Contact Details: Your full name, email address, phone number, and physical or mailing address.
  • Business Information: Your company name, website URL, target keywords, business goals, and current marketing strategies.
  • Account Credentials: Usernames, passwords, and other security information for account creation.
  • Financial Information: Billing details, credit card numbers, and payment history (processed securely through third-party payment gateways).

B. Non-Personal and Automated Information

As you navigate through and interact with our website, we use automatic data collection technologies to collect certain information about your equipment, browsing actions, and patterns. This non-personal data helps us optimize our website for better search engine rankings and user experience. This includes:

  • Technical Data: Your Internet Protocol (IP) address, browser type and version, time zone setting, operating system, and platform.
  • Usage Data: Details of your visits to our website, including traffic data, location data, logs, the specific pages you view, the links you click, and other communication data.
  • Referral Data: The website that referred you to BoostSERP, helping us understand our most effective marketing channels.

2. How We Use Your Information

At BoostSERP, we believe in data-driven results, but we also believe in data minimization. We only use the information we collect for specific, legitimate business purposes, which include:

  • Service Delivery: To provide you with the SEO campaigns, site audits, and marketing services you have requested, and to manage your account.
  • Communication: To contact you regarding your projects, respond to your inquiries, provide customer support, and send administrative information such as updates to our terms or policies.
  • Website Improvement: To present our website and its contents effectively, personalize your user experience, and analyze user behavior to improve our site's layout and functionality.
  • Marketing and Promotions: With your explicit consent, we may use your email address to send you industry insights, SEO tips, promotional offers, and company news. You can opt-out of these communications at any time.
  • Security and Fraud Prevention: To maintain the safety and security of our website, prevent fraudulent transactions, and monitor against cyber threats or unauthorized access.
  • Legal Compliance: To comply with applicable laws, regulations, and legal processes, or to protect the rights, property, and safety of BoostSERP, our clients, and others.

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

Trust is the foundation of our client relationships. BoostSERP does not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third-party marketers. However, we may share your data in the following carefully controlled circumstances:

  • Service Providers: We may share your information with trusted third-party vendors and service providers who perform services on our behalf. This includes payment processors, email delivery services, web hosting companies, and premium SEO tool providers (such as Google Analytics or Ahrefs integrations). These third parties are bound by strict confidentiality agreements.
  • Business Transfers: If BoostSERP is involved in a merger, acquisition, restructuring, or sale of assets, your personal information may be transferred as part of that business transaction. We will notify you via email or a prominent notice on our website of any change in ownership.
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose your information if required to do so by law, court order, or governmental regulation, or if we believe in good faith that such action is necessary to defend our legal rights.

4. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

To ensure our website operates smoothly and to enhance your user experience, BoostSERP utilizes cookies, web beacons, and similar tracking technologies.

  • What are Cookies? Cookies are small text files placed on your device by a web server. They allow our website to remember your actions and preferences over time.
  • How We Use Them: We use essential cookies to keep our site secure and functional. We also use analytical cookies to understand how visitors interact with our content, which helps us refine our own SEO and user interface.
  • Your Control: You have full control over your cookie preferences. You can set your browser to refuse all or some browser cookies, or to alert you when cookies are being sent. Please note that if you disable or refuse cookies, some parts of the BoostSERP website may become inaccessible or fail to function properly.

5. Data Security

We take the security of your data as seriously as we take your search engine rankings. BoostSERP implements a robust variety of physical, electronic, and administrative security measures to maintain the safety of your personal information.

These measures include SSL (Secure Socket Layer) encryption for data transmission, secure servers, firewalls, and strict access controls limited only to authorized personnel who require the information to perform their specific job duties.

Disclaimer: While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your personal information, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. Therefore, we cannot guarantee its absolute security, and you provide your information at your own risk.

6. Your Data Protection Rights (GDPR & CCPA Compliance)

Depending on your geographic location, you may have specific rights regarding your personal data under laws such as the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) in Europe or the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). BoostSERP extends these fundamental privacy rights to all our users:

  • The Right to Access: You have the right to request copies of your personal data that we hold.
  • The Right to Rectification: You have the right to request that we correct any information you believe is inaccurate or complete information you believe is incomplete.
  • The Right to Erasure (Right to be Forgotten): You have the right to request that we erase your personal data, under certain conditions.
  • The Right to Restrict Processing: You have the right to request that we restrict the processing of your personal data, under certain conditions.
  • The Right to Data Portability: You have the right to request that we transfer the data we have collected to another organization, or directly to you.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact our privacy team using the information provided at the bottom of this policy. We have 30 days to respond to your request.
