Project Overview

Phonebot is an Australian eCommerce powerhouse specializing in refurbished smartphones, tablets, and electronics. Operating in a hyper-competitive market dominated by global retail giants, the brand faced a significant challenge: standing out in search results for high-intent queries.

The objective was clear: develop a sustainable, data-driven organic growth strategy that would not only increase visibility but also drive high-conversion traffic to key product and category pages.

Verified Search Console Data

The SEO Challenges

Scaling an eCommerce site in the refurbished tech niche comes with unique hurdles:

  • Extreme Competition: Ranking against major Australian retailers and global marketplaces.
  • Technical Debt: Resolving crawlability and indexation issues inherent in large product catalogs.
  • CTR Optimization: Converting existing impressions into clicks through psychological listing optimization.

Strategic Implementation

1. Technical SEO Revamp

We initiated a deep-dive audit to identify bottlenecks in crawl budget and sitemap distribution. By optimizing internal linking and resolving XML sitemap errors, we ensured Google prioritized the most important category pages.

2. High-Intent Keyword Targeting

Research focused on "Buyer-Intent" keywords. We moved beyond generic terms, targeting specific Australian queries like "refurbished iPhones Melbourne" and "used Samsung S23 Australia," capturing users further down the sales funnel.
