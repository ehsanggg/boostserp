Where Have I Been?

For the past few months, I have been fully focused on scaling several high-impact client projects. Instead of sharing frequent updates, I dedicated my time to deep research, experimentation, and execution to achieve measurable growth for these businesses.

One of the most exciting projects involved working with a fast-growing SaaS platform operating in a highly competitive digital space. The goal was to improve the platform’s organic visibility, brand authority, and search performance while adapting to the latest changes in the search ecosystem.

Breaking Down the SEO Strategy

Growing a SaaS platform today requires a multi-layered SEO strategy that goes beyond simple keyword targeting. Our approach focused on combining traditional SEO foundations with modern AI-driven search optimization techniques.

Capturing Emerging Search Behaviors (AIO & GEO)

One of the biggest shifts in modern search is the rise of AI-generated search results and conversational search experiences. Google’s AI Overviews (AIO) and generative search systems are changing how users discover information.

To capitalize on this shift, we implemented a strategy focused on Generative Engine Optimization (GEO), which ensures that content is optimized not only for traditional search results but also for AI-powered answers.

This involved structuring content with clear topic coverage, structured headings, and concise answers to increase the likelihood of appearing in AI-generated summaries.

Continuous Content Resilience

Publishing content once and leaving it untouched is no longer effective. We implemented a continuous content resilience strategy, regularly auditing existing content to ensure accuracy and alignment with Google’s latest guidelines.

Existing pages were refreshed with improved structure and enhanced keyword targeting to better satisfy user intent and strengthen topical authority.

Data-Driven SEO Optimization

Using tools like Google Search Console, we monitored keyword rankings and user engagement metrics to identify high-potential keywords and discover new growth opportunities.

Pages with high impressions but low click-through rates were optimized with improved titles and meta descriptions to attract more clicks and drive higher quality traffic.
