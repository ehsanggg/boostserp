Project Overview

TeamFlow, a B2B SaaS platform for remote team management, faced a common startup hurdle: having a superior product while remaining invisible to potential customers. With a starting Domain Rating of 12, the site lacked the ranking power to compete for high-intent keywords like "remote team collaboration software."

Through a multi-phased authority building campaign, we transformed TeamFlow into a dominant niche player, scaling their DR to 36 and securing over 2,200 unique linking domains in just a few months.
