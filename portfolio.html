Frequently Asked Questions

Compiled from popular Quora & Reddit discussions

Are directory submissions still good for SEO in 2026?

Yes, but only if done correctly. Spammy, automated submissions using bots can actively harm your site. However, high-quality, manual submissions to curated startup, SaaS, and niche-specific directories build foundational trust with Google. They help establish your business entity and provide a safe, diverse base for your backlink profile.

Why shouldn't I just use cheap freelancers on Fiverr?

Many massive cheap gigs use automated tools (like GSA or scrapebox) that blast your link to irrelevant, toxic domains. This can result in manual penalties from Google. Our service is 100% manual. We hand-pick directories relevant to SaaS/AI, carefully fill out every field, optimize your descriptions, and ensure compliance with each directory's strict manual review process.

How long does it take to see results from Guest Posts?

Guest posting is a mid-to-long term strategy. While the posts themselves typically go live within our 3-5 day delivery window, Google needs time to index the pages, crawl the links, and pass the authority to your domain. You can usually expect to see positive movement in domain authority (DR/UR) and keyword rankings within 3 to 6 weeks.

Do you guarantee the DR (Domain Rating) for the guest post sites?

Absolutely. Depending on the plan you choose, we guarantee placement on sites that meet or exceed the stated Ahrefs Domain Rating (e.g., DR 30+). We also filter out sites with inflated metrics, ensuring you get placements on real websites with actual organic traffic.
